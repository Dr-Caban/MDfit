About the Show

Lone Soulja is a podcast rooted in Bahamian culture — from Nassau to the Family Islands. Each episode brings honest conversation about life, hustle, music, faith, and what it means to stand on your own.

Whether you're home in the 242 or part of the diaspora, pull up a chair. This is your corner.

  • 🎙️ Unfiltered conversations
  • 🌊 Island life & culture
  • 🥁 Music, Junkanoo & creatives
  • 💪 Hustle & self-made stories
