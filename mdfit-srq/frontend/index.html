The MD Fit Method

Treat the cause, not the symptom.

Pain is usually a muscle imbalance, tension or a faulty movement pattern. Fix that and the pain has nothing left to hold on to.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Pain and movement evaluation: posture, gait, flexibility and muscle balance, to find the real source.

  2. 02

    Release

    Hands-on therapy to reduce tension and get restricted muscles working again.

  3. 03

    Correct

    Neuromuscular re-education to retrain the movement pattern that caused the problem.

  4. 04

    Strengthen

    Progressive strength work so the fix lasts, and you get back to the things you love.
