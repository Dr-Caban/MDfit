The MD Fit Method

Five steps, in this order.

Each step builds on the one before it. Skip one and the body compensates, and compensation is where pain comes from.

  1. 01

    Range of motion

    Without the right range of motion you never get a proper contraction, and the body keeps compensating.

  2. 02

    Stability

    Control at every point of that range, especially the parts you use every day.

  3. 03

    Mobility

    Moving through your full range with control, and keeping it stable the whole way.

  4. 04

    Strength

    Maximum load, moved in a single plane.

  5. 05

    Dynamic strength

    Moving load through multiple planes, without pain.

How it works

From your first visit to the last.

  1. 01

    Assess

    A full evaluation of pain and movement: posture, gait, range, stability and muscle balance, to find the real source.

  2. 02

    Release

    Hands-on therapy to reduce tension and get restricted muscles working again.

  3. 03

    Correct

    Neuromuscular re-education to retrain the movement pattern behind the problem.

  4. 04

    Homework

    Every visit ends with homework for the days before your next one. Progress and you get new homework; not there yet and you keep going with the current set.

  5. 05

    Strengthen

    Progressive strength work so the fix lasts and you get back to what you love.
